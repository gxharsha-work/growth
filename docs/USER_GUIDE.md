# Growth beta: a one-page guide for team leads

Growth shows each team as a small village. It reads your team's Jira and calendar every day and turns them into a health score. It never shows anyone's individual tickets or meetings.

## 1. Sign in

- Go to **growth-3it.pages.dev** and enter your work email. Cloudflare sends you a one-time PIN.
- If you see "not allowed", ask the Growth admin to add your email and role.

## 2. Pick your team and week

- Choose your team in the list at the top right.
- Drag the **Week** slider at the bottom to look back in time.

## 3. Read the village

- Click **How to read your village** (bottom left) for the full legend.
- **Score (0 to 100):** built from the connected signals. A dash means there was not enough data that week.
- **Weather:** sun is healthy (80+), clouds mean watch (60 to 79), rain means a low score or an early warning.
- **Badges** show where each number comes from: **Live** (your real tools), **Simulated** (demo data) or **No data** (not connected, left out of the score).
- **Baseline and Cohort** explain the early-warning check and why your team is compared with its peers.

## 4. Act on an early warning

- A red banner appears when this week's score falls below 85% of your team's average over the previous 3 weeks.
- The banner shows the exact numbers and the signals that dropped most.
- Click **Get AI coaching suggestion** for one concrete next step. The score and numbers come from Growth's own calculation; the AI only words the suggestion.

## 5. Manage your roster and consent

- Open **Roster** in the right panel. It shows how many members have opted in, for example "4 of 6".
- Tick each member who has agreed to take part, or use **Import CSV** with the columns `name, role, team, consented`.
- Growth scores a team only after at least 3 members have opted in. Unticking someone takes effect right away.

Something wrong or confusing? Click **Report an issue** (bottom left). It saves your note with the team, the week and the app version.
