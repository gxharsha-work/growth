# E2 results - seeded-scenario evaluation of the early-warning detector

Generated by `scripts/validation/simulate.mjs`. 40 teams per scenario cell (80 null teams), 52 weeks each, seeds disjoint between DEV and HELD-OUT. Re-running reproduces every number.

**Self-checks passed:**
- deterministic: same seeds reproduce identical teams
- parametrised baseline detector == production isEarlyWarning on all dev series
- dev and held-out seed ranges are disjoint
- common random numbers: same team at different noise scales is highly correlated
